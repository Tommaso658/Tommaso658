### About Me

I am a graduate student in Mathematical Engineering, specializing in Quantitative Finance at Politecnico di Milano.

My academic interests lie at the intersection of mathematics, statistics, and financial markets, with a particular focus on probability, stochastic modeling, derivatives pricing, and financial risk management.

I recently completed an internship at **Intesa Sanpaolo**, working in Counterparty Risk, where I gained exposure to derivatives risk measurement, counterparty exposure methodologies, and quantitative risk analysis.

I enjoy exploring the mathematical foundations of financial models and understanding how theoretical concepts can be applied to real-world financial problems.

### Selected Academic Projects

**1. Multi-Name Credit Product Pricing (MBS/CDO)**

Pricing and calibration of multi-name credit products using double-t Student, Vasicek, and Gaussian copula models, including Large Homogeneous Portfolio approximations and Karhunen–Loève expansion.

**2. Asset Swap, CDS Bootstrap & Credit Simulation**

Financial Engineering project covering asset swap valuation, credit default swap curve bootstrapping, credit simulation, and structured product pricing.

**3. Portfolio VaR, Expected Shortfall & Counterparty Risk**

Quantitative risk measurement and derivatives pricing using historical, Gaussian, bootstrap, and Monte Carlo methodologies.

**4. FinTech Portfolio Replication**

Portfolio replication and allocation using Elastic Net, Ridge Regression, Kalman Filtering, and Hierarchical Risk Parity.
